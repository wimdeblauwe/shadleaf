Input Group

Composition

Section titled “Composition”

The group draws the border, and its control has none of its own: the group shows the control’s focus ring, its error and its read-only look. A disabled control fades the whole group. A click on an addon, outside a button or link in it, focuses the control.

Align

Section titled “Align”

Write the addons after the control, so the control comes first in the tab order and is read before them; align on the addon places it. inline-start, the default, puts it before the control on its line:

inline-end

Section titled “inline-end”

block-start

Section titled “block-start”

block-start puts the addon above the control, across the whole group. border draws a line between the addon and the control. In such a row an sl:input-group-text takes the free space, so what follows it sits at the end.

block-end

Section titled “block-end”

Text

Section titled “Text”

sl:input-group-text holds muted text: a currency, a unit, a domain. The input still needs a name of its own, as the text is not its label: aria-label="Amount in US dollars".

Button

Section titled “Button”

sl:input-group-button is an sl:button, ghost and xs by default, with sizes that fit inside the group: xs, sm, icon-xs and icon-sm. An icon button needs an aria-label. As every sl:button, it does not submit unless you write type="submit".

Kbd

Section titled “Kbd”

The key is decoration: say the shortcut with aria-keyshortcuts on the input, and hide the addon from screen readers.

Loading

Section titled “Loading”

loading on the button shows a spinner, disables it and announces the busy state.

In a field

Section titled “In a field”

Put th:field on the field, as for an input. The sl:input-group-input (or sl:input-group-textarea) inside the group takes it: Spring sets its id, name and value, the label points at it, and it is described by the field’s description and error.

Outside a field, write th:field on the sl:input-group-input itself.

Invalid

Section titled “Invalid”

When the field has errors, the input marks itself aria-invalid="true", and the group draws the destructive border and ring.

Disabled

Section titled “Disabled”

Read-only

Section titled “Read-only”

As a read-only input: the group gets a muted background, and the value can still be focused, selected and copied.

Attributes

Section titled “Attributes”

sl:input-group

Section titled “sl:input-group”

sl:input-group declares no props; every attribute passes through.

sl:input-group-addon

Section titled “sl:input-group-addon”
AttributeValuesDefaultDescription
aligninline-start inline-end block-start block-endinline-startWhere the addon goes: before or after the control on its line, or above or below it across the whole group.
borderboolean—With align block-start or block-end, a dividing line between the addon and the control, with matching spacing.

Every prop can also be set from an expression: th:align="${...}". Any other attribute passes through to the rendered element, and class is appended.

Section titled “sl:input-group-text”

sl:input-group-text declares no props; every attribute passes through.

sl:input-group-button

Section titled “sl:input-group-button”
AttributeValuesDefaultDescription
variantdefault outline ghost destructive secondary linkghostVisual style of the button, as on sl:button.
sizexs sm icon-xs icon-smxsSize of the button. The icon sizes are square, for a button that holds only an icon, and need an aria-label.

Every prop can also be set from an expression: th:variant="${...}". Any other attribute passes through to the rendered element, and class is appended. An aria-label or aria-labelledby is required when size is icon-xs or icon-sm; rendering fails without one.
