A stack of sections, each with a heading that shows or hides its content. Built on the browser's details element, so it needs no JavaScript: the keyboard, the open state, and find-in-page opening a section that holds a match all come from the browser. Holds sl:accordion-items, each with a sl:accordion-trigger and a sl:accordion-content. single: opening a section closes the one that was open (the items share a details name). multiple: sections open and close on their own. The group name the items of a single accordion share. Must be unique on the page. Without it, each render generates one (sl-accordion- and eight random characters).
