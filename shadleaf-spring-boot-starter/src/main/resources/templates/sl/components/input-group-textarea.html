The textarea of a sl:input-group: a sl:textarea without a border of its own, as the group draws it, that grows with its content where the browser supports it. Addons go above or below it (align block-start or block-end). The content is the initial text, unless th:field sets it. Every attribute that is not a prop passes through; class is appended to textarea. With th:field, the textarea is marked aria-invalid when its field has errors. In a sl:field, it takes the field's th:field, id and aria-describedby.
