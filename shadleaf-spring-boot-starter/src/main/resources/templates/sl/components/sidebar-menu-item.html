One entry of a sl:sidebar-menu: an li holding a sl:sidebar-menu-button, and optionally a sl:sidebar-menu-badge or a sl:sidebar-menu-action beside it. With a badge, the button is described by it (aria-describedby), so a screen reader says "Inbox, link, 12". The item's id. Its badge gets <id>-badge; without an id, each render generates one (sl-sidebar-menu-item- and eight random characters).
