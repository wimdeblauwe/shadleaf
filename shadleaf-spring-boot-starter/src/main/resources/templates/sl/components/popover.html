Rich content in a panel that opens from a button, on the browser's Popover API: it sits above the page next to its trigger, and Escape, a click outside or moving the focus out of it closes it. Holds a sl:popover-trigger and a sl:popover-content. Renders no element of its own. The popover's id, which the trigger points at with popovertarget. The title and description ids derive from it.
