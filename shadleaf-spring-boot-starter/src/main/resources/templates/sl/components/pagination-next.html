The link to the next page: a sl:pagination-link with a chevron and the text "Next" (the message sl.pagination.next), which content of its own replaces. Its name is "Go to next page" (sl.pagination.next.label); an aria-label replaces it. On a narrow screen only the chevron shows. There is no next page: the link loses its href, gets aria-disabled and leaves the tab order. Next
