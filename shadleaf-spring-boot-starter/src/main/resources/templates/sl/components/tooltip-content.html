The text of a sl:tooltip (role="tooltip"), after its trigger. Keep it short and plain: it cannot hold links or buttons, and touch screens never show it. It flips to the other side when there is no room. In a tooltip with mode="label" it is hidden from assistive technology (aria-hidden). The side of the trigger it shows on. right and left are the end and the start of a line. How it lines up with the trigger along that side.
