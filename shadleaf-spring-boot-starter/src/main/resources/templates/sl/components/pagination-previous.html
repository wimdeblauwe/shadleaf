The link to the previous page: a sl:pagination-link with a chevron and the text "Previous" (the message sl.pagination.previous), which content of its own replaces. Its name is "Go to previous page" (sl.pagination.previous.label); an aria-label replaces it. On a narrow screen only the chevron shows. There is no previous page: the link loses its href, gets aria-disabled and leaves the tab order. Previous
