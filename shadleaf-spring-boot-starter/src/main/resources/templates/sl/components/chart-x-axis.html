The category or time axis of a sl:chart, along the bottom: its ticks are the values of key in the rows, without tick or axis lines (as shadcn's). Under dir="rtl" it runs from right to left. Draws no element of its own on the page: the chart reads it. The property of the rows that holds the category (month) or the date. How the ticks are written, with Intl in the page's language. Numbers: number, integer, compact (1.2K), percent (0.25 is 25%), currency (with currency). Dates: day (Apr 1), month-short (Apr), month (April), month-year (Apr 2024), date, time. Without one, the values as they are. The ISO 4217 code (EUR) for format="currency". Space in pixels between the ticks and the plot. The least space in pixels between two ticks; Recharts leaves out ticks that would come closer, as on a long time axis.
