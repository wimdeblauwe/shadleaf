A month calendar to pick a date or a range of dates, drawn over native date inputs: the inputs are its value, so th:field binds it and the form submits ISO dates (2026-10-23). Without content, it renders one date input itself, which takes every attribute that is not a prop except class (th:field, name, required, min, max), and in a sl:field the field's th:field, id and aria-describedby. For a range, give it two sl:input type="date"s as content, the start and the end; its attributes then go to the calendar. slCalendar hides the inputs and draws the month; without JavaScript the inputs show as they are. The month's caption: its name, or selects for the month and year, the month, or the year. How many months show side by side (stacked on a narrow screen). Show the days of the previous and next month that fill the first and last week. Show the week numbers, counted as the page's locale counts them (ISO weeks in most of Europe). The first day of the week: 0 is Sunday, 1 Monday, up to 6. By default the page's locale decides. The month shown first, as 2026-10. By default the month of the chosen date (or of the start of the range), or else the current month. The first month the calendar navigates to, as 2026-01; also the first year of the year select. By default the month of the inputs' min, or else 100 years ago. The last month the calendar navigates to, as 2026-12; also the last year of the year select. By default the month of the inputs' max, or else 10 years from now. Days that cannot be picked, comma-separated: a date (2026-12-25), a range (2026-12-24..2026-12-31) or a day of the week (sat, sun). The inputs' min and max disable the days outside them too. Only the calendar knows: check the date on the server as well.
