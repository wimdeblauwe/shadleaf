A group of related fields or options under a sl:field-legend, as a fieldset. With th:field, the options inside share it: radio buttons in a sl:radio-group, or checkboxes each in a sl:field of their own (which then need no th:field and get Spring's numbered ids, toppings1). A sl:field-description and sl:field-error directly inside describe the fieldset, and it gets data-invalid when the binding has errors.
