The icon button that collapses and expands the sl:sidebar on a desktop and opens its panel on a phone. Named "Toggle sidebar" (sl.sidebar.trigger; an aria-label replaces it); content of its own replaces the icon. Without JavaScript it is hidden on a desktop, where it could not do anything, and still opens the panel on a phone. Any other attribute passes through to the sl:button. The id of the sidebar it toggles. Visual style of the button, as on sl:button. Size of the button: one of sl:button's icon sizes.
