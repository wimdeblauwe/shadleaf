A header bar: a header laying out its content in a row. Across the top of the page (inside sl:sidebar-provider, before sl:sidebar-inset) it is the header layout's: a sl:sidebar-trigger that opens the menu on a phone, the application's name, the sl:sidebar with placement="header" as a row of links, and the end slot. At the top of sl:sidebar-inset it is the page's header in the sidebar layout: the trigger, a vertical sl:separator and a sl:breadcrumb. The end slot (search, the theme toggle, the signed-in user) goes to the end of the row.
