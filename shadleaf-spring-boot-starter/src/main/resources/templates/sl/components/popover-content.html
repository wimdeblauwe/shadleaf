The panel of a sl:popover, next to its trigger in the top layer. It is a non-modal dialog: name it with a sl:popover-title (in a sl:popover-header) or an aria-label. When it opens, focus moves to its first control. It flips to the other side when there is no room. The side of the trigger it opens on. right and left are the end and the start of a line: on a right-to-left page a right popover opens on the left. How it lines up with the trigger along that side.
