A link to one page, drawn as a ghost button, or an outline one for the page shown now (active). Give it an href or th:href, and the page number as content. Whether it is the page shown now: aria-current="page" and the outline look. Size of the link, as on sl:button: square by default, for a number. There is nowhere to go: the link loses its href, gets aria-disabled and leaves the tab order, as a disabled sl:button as="a".
