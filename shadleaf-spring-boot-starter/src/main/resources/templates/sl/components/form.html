A form. Every attribute that is not a prop (th:object, th:action, method, novalidate, hx-*) passes through. With validate, every control in a sl:field or sl:field-set with th:field is validated on the server while the user edits it: it posts the whole form to that URL with htmx 4 and swaps only its own field from the response (see sl:validate). A field whose errors come from a rule over several fields names them in its depends-on. The URL of the endpoint that validates the form object and renders the form again, such as th:validate="@{/bookings/validate}". Needs htmx 4; without htmx the form posts as usual.
