Links to the other pages of a list, in a nav. Given the Spring Data Page it shows (th:page="${people}") and no content of its own, it renders them all: Previous, the first and the last page, the current page with siblings on each side, an ellipsis for the pages left out, and Next. Every link keeps the request's other parameters (sort, size, filters) and has a stable id, so htmx can give the focus back after a swap. A Slice, which does not know how many pages there are, gets Previous and Next only. Without Spring Data, pass current and total instead. With content of its own it is the frame for a hand-written pagination of sl:pagination-content, -item, -link, -previous, -next and -ellipsis, as in shadcn/ui. The Spring Data Page or Slice shown now. Without a page: the page shown now, counting from 1. Without a page: the number of pages. How many pages to show on each side of the current one. The @Qualifier of the controller's Pageable, for a second list on the page: its links use members_page instead of page, and their ids start with members-.
