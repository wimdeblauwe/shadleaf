A chart drawn by Recharts in the browser: bars, lines and areas over a category or time axis, with a grid, axes, a tooltip and a legend. Pass the rows in th:data and put the parts inside, in the order Recharts draws them: sl:chart-grid, sl:chart-x-axis, sl:chart-y-axis, sl:chart-tooltip, sl:chart-legend, then one sl:chart-bar, sl:chart-line or sl:chart-area per series (only bars give a bar chart, a mix a composed chart). Needs JavaScript: without it the chart's skeleton stays. The chart is 16:9; for a fixed height, give it one in your CSS with aspect-ratio: auto. Every attribute that is not a prop passes through to the element. The rows: a list (or a Spring Data Page) of objects, records or maps, one per category or point in time. Each row holds the category (month) and a number per series (desktop, mobile). Serialised with Jackson: dates as ISO strings, which the named date formats read by their Java type. The chart's accessible name, such as its title: what a screen reader announces when the chart takes the focus. Space in pixels left and right of the plot, so the first and last tick labels fit (12 suits a line or area chart). The time zone (an IANA id such as Europe/Brussels) instants in the data are shown in: Instant, OffsetDateTime and ZonedDateTime. Defaults to the request's (Spring's LocaleContextHolder). A LocalDate or LocalDateTime is shown as written, in any zone.
