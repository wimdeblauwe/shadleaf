Where toasts show: short messages in a corner of the page that close themselves, such as "Member deleted". Put one at the end of the layout's body. Toasts get there three ways: as sl:toasts inside it, which the server renders (e.g. from a flash attribute after a redirect); from an sl-toast event, which an htmx response sends with the HX-Trigger header; or swapped in out of band. A screen reader announces each toast, and toasts show above an open modal dialog and can be used there. Needs Alpine. The toaster's id, which an out-of-band swap targets (hx-swap-oob="beforeend:#toaster"). The corner or edge the toasts show in. right and left are the end and the start of a line. How long a toast shows, in milliseconds, unless it sets its own. The time stops while the pointer is on the toasts, while one of them has the focus and while the page is hidden. 0 keeps toasts until they are closed. How many toasts show at once, the newest ones. Older ones wait, with their time stopped, and show again when a newer one closes.
