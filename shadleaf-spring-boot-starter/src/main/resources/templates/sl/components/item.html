A row of content: an sl:item-media (an icon, an avatar or an image), an sl:item-content with an sl:item-title and an sl:item-description, and sl:item-actions; sl:item-header and sl:item-footer take a line of their own. Group items in an sl:item-group, which makes them a list. For a form control with its label, use sl:field instead. Visual style: default has no border or background, outline a border, muted a muted background. Size of the item: its padding, gaps and the size of an image in its media. Element to render: a div, or an anchor (a, give it an href) that makes the whole item a link, tinted on hover. A link item holds no other links or buttons.
