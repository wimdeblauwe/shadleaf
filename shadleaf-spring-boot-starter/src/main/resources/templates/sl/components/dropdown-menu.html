A menu of actions or links that opens from a button, on the browser's Popover API: it sits above the page, and Escape or a click outside closes it. Holds a sl:dropdown-menu-trigger and a sl:dropdown-menu-content with the items. Renders no element of its own. Needs Alpine for the arrow keys, typeahead and closing after an item is chosen. The menu's id, which the trigger points at with popovertarget. Any other button can open it that way too.
