Text, an icon, a sl:kbd or sl:input-group-buttons beside or around the control of a sl:input-group. Clicking it, outside a button or link, focuses the control. Write it after the control: align places it. Where the addon goes: before or after the control on its line, or above or below it across the whole group. With align block-start or block-end, a dividing line between the addon and the control, with matching spacing.
