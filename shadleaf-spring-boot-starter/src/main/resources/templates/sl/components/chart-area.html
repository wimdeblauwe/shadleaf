One series of a sl:chart, drawn as a filled area under a line through the rows. Draws no element of its own on the page: the chart reads it. The property of the rows that holds this series' numbers (desktop): letters, digits, _ and -, as it also names the colour property --color-<key>. The series' name in the tooltip and the legend, typically a message (th:label="#{visitors.desktop}"). Defaults to the key. Its colour, any CSS colour (var(--chart-3), #2563eb). Defaults to the chart tokens in turn: the first series var(--chart-1), the second var(--chart-2), and so on (after the fifth, again from the first). A different colour in the dark theme; color is then the light one (the two go into light-dark()). How the line runs between the points: smooth (natural, monotone), straight (linear) or in steps. Stack this series on the others with the same stack id (any name, such as a). Draw the line on top of the area dashed (the fill stays as it is). The legend and the tooltip show the series dashed as well. The key of the series this dashed area continues (actual), as on sl:chart-line: it starts at that series' last number, and the tooltip does not list this series at that last row. Needs dashed. The opacity of the fill, from 0 to 1.
