Sheet

Built from parts

Section titled “Built from parts”

A sheet is a dialog on an edge of the screen, for content that complements the page: filters, settings, the details of a row. It is <sl:sheet> plus the parts you need, as in shadcn/ui: <sl:sheet-header> (holding <sl:sheet-title> and <sl:sheet-description>), your own content and <sl:sheet-footer>.

Everything the dialog page says holds for a sheet: it is the browser’s <dialog> element, buttons open and close it with commandfor and command, the page behind it is inert and does not scroll, Escape and a click outside close it, focus returns to the button that opened it, and open shows one the server renders (an htmx swap). The title and description name and describe it, with ids derived from its id.

The content between the header and the footer gets the sheet’s inline padding, so it lines up with them. The footer stays at the bottom of a sheet on the side of the screen, however little is above it. Content taller than the screen scrolls inside the sheet.

Side

Section titled “Side”

side is the edge it slides in from: top, right (the default), bottom or left. A sheet on the top or bottom is as wide as the screen and as tall as its content; one on the right or left is as tall as the screen and three quarters of its width, up to a maximum.

right and left are the end and the start of a line, as everywhere in Shadleaf: on a right-to-left page (dir="rtl") a right sheet opens on the left and slides in from there.

The sheet fades in and slides a short distance from its edge. With reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) it only fades.

Close buttons

Section titled “Close buttons”

The close button in the corner is on by default; close-button="false" leaves it out. <sl:sheet-footer close-button> adds an outline “Close” button after the footer’s own actions.

Attributes

Section titled “Attributes”

sl:sheet

Section titled “sl:sheet”
AttributeValuesDefaultDescription
idstringrequiredThe sheet's id, which the buttons that open and close it point at with commandfor. The title and description ids derive from it.
sidetop right bottom leftrightThe edge it slides in from. right and left are the end and the start of a line: on a right-to-left page a right sheet opens on the left.
openboolean—Open the sheet as soon as it is on the page, for a sheet the server renders in answer to a request (an htmx swap). Needs Alpine.
close-buttonbooleantrueA close button (an x) in the corner.

Every prop can also be set from an expression: th:id="${...}". Any other attribute passes through to the rendered element, and class is appended.

JavaScript

Section titled “JavaScript”

A sheet uses the dialog’s Alpine component, slDialog; see Dialog.

  • Most of it works without Alpine. Opening from a button, Escape, a click outside and the close buttons are the browser’s. Alpine is needed for open and the sl-dialog-close event, which closes a sheet as it closes a dialog.
  • It needs no Alpine plugin.
  • Keep your own x-data off <sl:sheet>: it would replace slDialog. Put it on an element inside.
