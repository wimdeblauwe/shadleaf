Item

Built from parts

Section titled “Built from parts”

An item is <sl:item> plus the parts you need: <sl:item-media> (an icon, an avatar or an image), <sl:item-content> (holding <sl:item-title> and <sl:item-description>) and <sl:item-actions>. <sl:item-header> and <sl:item-footer> take a line of their own, above and below the rest. <sl:item-group> makes several items a list.

An item only lays out what you put in it. For a form control with its label and description, such as a checkbox or a switch, use <sl:field> instead.

Variant

Section titled “Variant”

variant is default (no border or background), outline or muted.

Size

Section titled “Size”

size is default, sm or xs. It sets the padding and the gaps, the size of an image in the media and, in a group, the gap between the items.

Icon

Section titled “Icon”

<sl:item-media variant="icon"> sizes an icon. When the item has a description, the media lines up with the title instead of the middle of the item.

Avatar

Section titled “Avatar”

The default media shows its content as it is: an <sl:avatar>, or a few of them next to each other.

Image

Section titled “Image”

<sl:item-media variant="image"> crops an <img> to a square that follows the item’s size. The title already names the item, so the image can have an empty alt. A second <sl:item-content> after the first is as wide as its text, for a duration, a size or a price at the end.

Group

Section titled “Group”

<sl:item-group> stacks items with a gap that follows their size. It is a list (role="list") and every item in it a list item, so a screen reader announces how many there are.

<sl:item-separator> draws a line between two items, for items without a border. A group with separators has no gap of its own: the separator’s margins are the space between the items.

Header

Section titled “Header”

<sl:item-header> takes the full width above the rest of the item; an image in it is cropped to a square. With layout="grid" the group lays its items out in as many columns as fit the width; the width of a column comes from the skin.

Link

Section titled “Link”

as="a" makes the whole item a link: give it an href. It is tinted on hover and draws the skin’s focus ring. Its title and description are the link’s text, so keep them short, and put no other links or buttons in it: a link cannot hold them.

In a group a link item is wrapped in a div that is the list item, so the link keeps its role.

Attributes

Section titled “Attributes”

sl:item

Section titled “sl:item”
AttributeValuesDefaultDescription
variantdefault outline muteddefaultVisual style: default has no border or background, outline a border, muted a muted background.
sizedefault sm xsdefaultSize of the item: its padding, gaps and the size of an image in its media.
asdiv adivElement to render: a div, or an anchor (a, give it an href) that makes the whole item a link, tinted on hover. A link item holds no other links or buttons.

Every prop can also be set from an expression: th:variant="${...}". Any other attribute passes through to the rendered element, and class is appended.

sl:item-media

Section titled “sl:item-media”
AttributeValuesDefaultDescription
variantdefault icon imagedefaultdefault shows its content as it is (an sl:avatar, or a few of them), icon sizes an icon, image crops an &lt;img&gt; to a square that follows the item's size.

Every prop can also be set from an expression: th:variant="${...}". Any other attribute passes through to the rendered element, and class is appended.

sl:item-content

Section titled “sl:item-content”

sl:item-content declares no props; every attribute passes through.

sl:item-title

Section titled “sl:item-title”
AttributeValuesDefaultDescription
level1 2 3 4 5 6—Heading level. With it the title is a heading (role="heading" and aria-level) that screen reader users can jump to; without it, plain text. Pick the level that fits the page's outline.

Every prop can also be set from an expression: th:level="${...}". Any other attribute passes through to the rendered element, and class is appended.
