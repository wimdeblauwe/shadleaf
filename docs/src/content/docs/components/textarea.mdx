The textarea grows with its content in browsers that support `field-sizing`; elsewhere it keeps its height and
scrolls. `rows` sets a starting height.

## Initial text

With `th:field`, Spring fills the textarea from the field. Without it, the component's content is the initial text:

<ComponentPreview id="textarea--initial-text" />

## Invalid

As with the [input](../input/), a field with errors marks the textarea `aria-invalid="true"`.

<ComponentPreview id="textarea--invalid" />

## Disabled

As the [input](../input/#disabled): faded, not focusable and not submitted; lyra adds a grey background.

<ComponentPreview id="textarea--disabled" />

## Read-only

As the [input](../input/#read-only): a muted background and full text colour; it takes focus and is submitted.
