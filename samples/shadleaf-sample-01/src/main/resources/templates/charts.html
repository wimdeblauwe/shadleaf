Interactive Charts

Four sl:charts over the people of the tables, counted by the database (H2, with JPA queries). The series are the roles, one part per role with th:each; the months are YearMonths, which the named formats write in the page's language. The sidebar's links load this page with htmx, and the charts draw themselves when they arrive.
