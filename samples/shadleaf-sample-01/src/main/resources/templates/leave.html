A range of dates in a sl:calendar: its two date inputs, the first and the last day, each bind a field with th:field. The first click picks the first day, the second the last. Weekends, public holidays and the days before today cannot be picked (disabled-dates and the inputs' min), and the controller checks the same on the server. Without JavaScript the two date inputs show instead.
