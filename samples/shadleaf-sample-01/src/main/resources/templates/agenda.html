Interactive Team agenda

A sl:month-view: each day shows its events from a Map<LocalDate, List<String>> in the model, through the day slot. Every day links to ?date=…, which lists that day's events below. The previous and next month are links with ?month=…; the boosted body swaps the page with htmx, and without JavaScript they are plain links.
