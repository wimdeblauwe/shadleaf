Forms Trip

Two sl:comboboxes. The destination is one of some 250 countries, too many for the page: its search-url makes the combobox ask the server for the countries that match as you type, and the controller answers with the HTML of their sl:combobox-items (the countries fragment of this page). The page itself only holds the chosen country, so the combobox can show it. The languages are a multiple combobox with its options in the page: the chosen ones are chips, and th:field binds them to a list. Without JavaScript both are selects, the destination with only the chosen country. The daily budget is an sl:input-group in a field: the currency sits inside the input's border, and the field's label, description and error point at the input. How you travel is an sl:toggle-group in a field set: radio buttons drawn as joined buttons, which take the field set's th:field. Flexible dates is an sl:toggle bound to a boolean. Both work without JavaScript.
