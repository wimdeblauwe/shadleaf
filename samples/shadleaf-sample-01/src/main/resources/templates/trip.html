Forms Trip

Two sl:comboboxes. The destination is one of some 250 countries, too many for the page: its search-url makes the combobox ask the server for the countries that match as you type, and the controller answers with the HTML of their sl:combobox-items (the countries fragment of this page). The page itself only holds the chosen country, so the combobox can show it. The languages are a multiple combobox with its options in the page: the chosen ones are chips, and th:field binds them to a list. Without JavaScript both are selects, the destination with only the chosen country.
