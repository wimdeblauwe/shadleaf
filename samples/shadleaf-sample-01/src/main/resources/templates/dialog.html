From the server, with htmx

Team Shadleaf

Rename Rename the team Everyone on the team sees the new name.

Rename opens a popover with a form that posts with htmx: with errors the form comes back with them and the popover stays open; after a valid rename the response header HX-Trigger: sl-popover-close closes it and the name is updated.

Each member has a menu of row actions. Edit fetches a dialog into #modal-root, and <sl:dialog open> shows it. Save posts the form with htmx: with errors the form comes back with them and the dialog stays open; after a valid save the response header HX-Trigger: sl-dialog-close closes it and the row is updated, with the focus back on the row's menu button.

Delete fetches an alert dialog the same way. Its Delete button sends hx-delete; the response closes the alert dialog with the same event, shows a toast saying what happened (HX-Trigger: sl-toast), removes the row and moves the focus to the next row's menu button. Cancel, which has the focus when it opens, sends nothing.

Sort by is a menu of radio items: links that load the page sorted, which marks the chosen one. Invite member opens a sheet that is in the page. Its form posts with htmx and swaps itself: with errors the sheet stays open; after a valid invite an empty form comes back, the sheet closes, the new member is added to the table and a toast, swapped in out of band, says the invitation was sent.
