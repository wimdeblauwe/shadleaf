Interactive Dialog & Sheet

On the page

The dialog is in the page; the button opens it with commandfor and command="show-modal". Escape, a click outside and the close buttons close it. The button is an icon; its tooltip shows when the pointer rests on it or the keyboard focuses it.

From the server, with htmx

The team below is managed with htmx. Each member has a menu of row actions; the buttons above the table act on the whole team. Everything posts with htmx and swaps only what changed.

Dialogs

  • Edit (row menu) loads the member's form into the body of a dialog that is in the page (<sl:dialog-body>): the dialog opens at once, with a spinner until the form is in and the focus on its first field. With errors the form comes back with them and the dialog stays open; after a valid save HX-Trigger: sl-dialog-close closes it, the row is updated and the focus is back on the row's menu button.
  • Delete (row menu) fetches an alert dialog into #modal-root, where <sl:alert-dialog open> shows it, with the focus on Cancel, which sends nothing. Its Delete button sends hx-delete: the response closes the alert dialog, shows a toast (HX-Trigger: sl-toast), removes the row and moves the focus to the next row's menu button.

Sheets

  • Details (row menu) loads the member into the body of a sheet that is in the page (<sl:sheet-body>): the sheet opens at once and shows a spinner until the answer is in. The server takes a second to answer, and fails for Grace Hopper: then the sheet closes and a toast says the member could not be loaded.
  • Invite member opens a sheet whose form is in the page. It posts with htmx and swaps itself: with errors the sheet stays open; after a valid invite an empty form comes back, the sheet closes, the new member is added to the table and a toast, swapped in out of band, says the invitation was sent.

Menus and popovers

  • Sort by is a menu of radio items: links that load the page sorted, which marks the chosen one.
  • Rename opens a popover with a form that posts with htmx: with errors the form comes back with them and the popover stays open; after a valid rename HX-Trigger: sl-popover-close closes it and the name is updated.
