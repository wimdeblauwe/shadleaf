Forms Book a room

This form is validated on the server as you type. With <sl:form validate>, each control posts the form to /room-booking/validate while you edit it, and htmx 4 swaps in only that field. The rules are Bean Validation's, on the server; there are none in JavaScript.

The rules
  • Title: required, at most 60 characters.
  • Day: a weekday, today or later.
  • Start: between 08:00 and 18:00.
  • End: after the start, at most 4 hours later, and by 18:00. Depends on the start.
  • Room: required, and free at that time (see the bookings below). Depends on the day, the start and the end.
  • Attendees: at least 1, at most what the room seats. Depends on the room.
  • Catering: lunch is for 4 people or more. Depends on the attendees.
  • Your email: a valid address.
  • Notes: optional, at most 200 characters.

A field that depends on others declares them with depends-on, and is checked again when one of them changes, once it has a value itself. The submit validates everything again; without JavaScript, only the submit does.
